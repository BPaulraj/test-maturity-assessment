The maturity model

Five levels, from reactive to self-optimizing

Every team lands on this ladder independently for each of five dimensions — a team can be Defined on process while still Ad Hoc on AI adoption.

Full rubric: 03-Maturity-Model/Dimension-Rubrics.md

L1Ad Hoc / Reactive
L2Emerging / Repeatable
L3Defined / Standardized
L4Managed / Measured
L5Optimizing / Leading-edge

Process exists but inconsistently followed. Manual, unmeasured. Heroics save releases.
Standardised, consistently followed. Dashboards exist. Minimum acceptable steady state for Tier 1/2 teams.
Metrics actively drive release and investment decisions. Automation gates CI/CD. AI used in governed, bounded ways.
Source of org best practice. Predictive/AI-driven testing. Metrics used to prevent problems, not just find them.
Grading for leadership

Every team rolls up to one letter grade

Composite of the five dimension scores — but capped whenever a critical gap exists, so an average can never quietly hide real risk.

A
Level 4–5

Model team

Low release risk. Candidate to host org best practice and mentor other teams.

Posture: fund innovation & enablement role
B
Level 3

Meets the floor

Acceptable steady state — meets the policy minimum, not yet a differentiator.

Posture: fund targeted advancement
C
Level 2, or gated

Below the floor

Real release/quality risk. Includes any team gated down by a critical gap.

Posture: funded plan, executive visibility
D
Level 1

Foundational gaps

High risk. Needs COE-embedded support, not a self-service plan on paper.

Posture: immediate intervention
How the grade is built

Five weighted dimensions, evidence-gated

Scores require a linked artefact — a dashboard, repo, or pipeline — not a claim. No evidence caps the score regardless of narrative.

Detail: 02-Assessment-Questionnaire/Scoring-and-Weighting-Guide.md

Process 35%
Coverage 20%
Automation & Tooling 25%
AI 10%
Culture 10%
Process
Planning, design, execution, defects, environments
Coverage
Depth across all applicable testing types & levels
Automation & Tooling
Capability and capacity, cross-checked against the automation deep-dive
AI Adoption
Use-case breadth, gated by governance compliance
Competency, Governance & Culture
Skills, career path, whole-team ownership
Weakest-link gating overrides the average
A violated non-negotiable, a security/critical-coverage score of 0–1 on a Tier 1/2 team, or one dimension sitting at L1 while others sit at L4+ all cap the overall grade at Grade C — regardless of the composite number.
Beyond process, automation & AI

The 360° view — what else this covers

A maturity model that only scores the three headline areas gives a false sense of completeness. These are scored as first-class dimensions too.

Engineering & Environments

  • Test environment provisioning & parity
  • Test data masking & synthetic generation
  • Contract & chaos/resilience testing
  • Non-functional gates: performance, security, a11y

Quality Feedback Loops

  • Defect root-cause & escape analysis
  • Requirements-to-test traceability
  • Release retrospectives, not just sign-off
  • KPI trend reporting, not point-in-time snapshots

People & Culture

  • Skills matrix & individual development plans
  • SDET/QE career pathing
  • Whole-team quality ownership, not a QA silo
  • Psychological safety to delay a release

Governance & Risk

  • Tooling & license cost governance
  • AI data-classification & approval board
  • Risk-tiering, not one-size-fits-all standards
  • Exception tracking visible to leadership
